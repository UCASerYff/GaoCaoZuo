The runtime is redistributed under the CPython/PSF license and the included
third-party license notices. License texts and PYTHON.json were obtained from
the same release's aarch64-apple-darwin-pgo+lto-full.tar.zst distribution.
Only Python's interpreter and standard library required to run the adapter
are retained from this distribution. Separately licensed pyzipper and
PyCryptodome packages provide Unicode-password AES ZIP interoperability;
their notices and download hashes are in ../pyzipper and ../pycryptodomex.
pip, development headers, manuals, Tcl/Tk, GUI demos, and the
unused shared libpython are removed. No FastZip or other proprietary framework
has been copied. Interpreter source is available from the upstream CPython
and python-build-standalone projects.

The adapter uses /usr/lib/libarchive.2.dylib supplied by macOS. libarchive is
not copied into the distribution. Its upstream source and license:
https://github.com/libarchive/libarchive
https://github.com/libarchive/libarchive/blob/master/COPYING

acefile is separately redistributed in ArchiveBridge/acefile.py. See
../acefile/LICENSE.md and ../acefile/SOURCE.txt.
